## Goal of This Job
1. Remove debris sitting on top of the **right-hand speaker** that vibrates when notes are played.
2. Thorough dust cleanup.
3. Light application of electrical contact cleaner to contacts and PCBs.

---

## Required Tools & Materials

**Essential**
- Phillips screwdrivers (#1 and #2)
- Magnetic parts tray or labelled containers / ziplock bags
- Anti-static wrist strap (grounded to chassis)
- Plastic pry tools / spudgers / guitar picks
- Soft artist brushes or anti-static brushes
- Canned compressed air (electronics grade, with straw)
- Soft microfiber cloths + lint-free swabs
- Fine tweezers
- Bright flashlight / headlamp
- Phone/camera for photographing every step

**Cleaning**
- Residue-free electronics contact cleaner (e.g. DeoxIT, CRC QD, or equivalent)
- 99% isopropyl alcohol (IPA)
- Optional: low-suction vacuum with soft brush attachment

**Workspace**
- Clean, well-lit, static-controlled area
- Soft padded surface for the instrument and removed panels

## High-Level Disassembly Sequence
*(Always unplug power cord completely before starting. Discharge static from your body.)*

### 1. Control Panel Unit (~2 minutes)
- Remove the six (6) screws marked **[240]** located under the keybed.
- Lift the front of the side covers to open/raise the control panel unit.
- Remove the two (2) screws marked **[220A]**.
- The control panel unit (with side covers) can then be lifted away from the keybed.

**Cautions from service manual**
- Re-attach any filament tape exactly as found.
- Never reverse the orientation of flat cables.

### 2. Speaker Box Assembly (Right side)
After the control panel is removed, the right speaker box assembly becomes accessible.  
Remove the relevant screws securing Speaker Box Assembly R (references include **[180A/B]**, **[220B]**, **[170A/B]**, washers **[330]**, etc. – consult the service manual figures).

There are also Speaker Grille Assemblies that can be removed separately with fewer screws if the debris is reachable that way.

### 3. Speaker Driver Itself
Once the speaker box is free:
- Remove the four (4) screws marked **[40]**.
- The 13 cm speaker can then be taken out.
- Debris sitting on top of the right speaker should now be accessible.

*(Left and right speakers use the same method.)*

---

## Cleaning Procedure

1. **Debris removal**  
   Gently lift or vacuum the foreign material. Do **not** press or poke the speaker cone or surround.

2. **General dust**  
   - Brush first with soft brushes.  
   - Follow with short bursts of compressed air (hold can upright, keep distance).  
   - Use low-suction vacuum carefully if needed.

3. **Contacts & PCBs**  
   - Power fully disconnected.  
   - Light spray of residue-free contact cleaner on connectors, pots, switches, and board contacts.  
   - Use IPA on a swab for stubborn dirt.  
   - Avoid flooding boards or getting liquid into the speakers.  
   - Allow **complete drying** (several hours if necessary) before reassembly.

---

## Reassembly Tips
- Reverse the steps exactly.
- Double-check every flat-cable orientation.
- Restore filament tape to original positions.
- Do not overtighten screws.
- Take photos at every stage for reference.
- After powering on, test at low volume first and listen specifically for the previous vibration.

---

## Additional Notes
- Speakers: 13 cm × 2, driven by internal 30 W × 2 amplifiers.
- Work slowly and methodically. Label every screw group.
- If anything feels wrong during reassembly or testing, stop and seek professional help.
